My Top 5 Anime

  1. Attack on Titan

    Attack on Titan is one of my favorite anime because of its intense story, action, and unexpected plot twists. Eren deserved better!

  2. Naruto

    Naruto is one of my favorites because I enjoy watching the characters grow stronger and overcome difficult challenges and its very nostalgic.

  3. JJK

    JJK is a thrilling anime that combines supernatural elements with a unique take on the shonen genre. I also am a huge fan of Gojo.

  4. Demon Slayer

    Demon Slayer stands out to me because of its amazing animation, exciting fights, and emotional story between Nezuko and Tanjiro.

  5. Blue Exorcist

    Blue Exorcist is a unique anime that blends supernatural themes with a compelling story about good versus evil. The character development is exceptional.
